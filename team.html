Theron Carmine De Sousa has worked across leading global organisations, brands and governmental bodies, including a pivotal project helmed by the Dept of Investment and Public Asset Management (DIPAM).

Dr Ryan Michigan holds an MBA and PhD in Management from Columbia University, New York, a secondary MBA from the Indian Institute of Management, Ahmedabad, and an undergraduate degree in Physics from St Xavier's College, Mumbai.

He is a strategy, risk and analytics professional with multiple decades of experience across strategy, risk, marketing and analytics. He has worked closely with startups, FinTechs and major financial institutions in India, the United States and the Asia Pacific region.

Ryan also focused on consumer insight and data during his time with Boston Consulting in North America. He currently consults with OnDeck Australia, a fintech focused on small business finance across the Australian region.
